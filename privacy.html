Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Timetable Sync for ManageBac ("the extension") is a Chrome extension that copies your class timetable from ManageBac into your Google Calendar. It is made by an independent developer (contact: hello@qurious.im). This policy explains, in plain language, what it does with your information.

In short: the extension runs entirely on your computer. It has no server. The developer never receives your timetable, your calendar or your Google account details. Your timetable goes from ManageBac to your own Google Calendar and nowhere else.

What the extension reads

  • Your ManageBac timetable. When you click Sync to Google Calendar or Download .ics on a ManageBac page, the extension reads your timetable from ManageBac, week by week, using the ManageBac session you are already signed in to. It reads class names, dates, times, periods, rooms and, on student timetables, teacher names. It does not read any other part of ManageBac, such as grades, messages or files.
  • Your Google Calendar, only what it needs. With your permission, the extension looks up your main calendar's name and time zone (to show which account is connected) and the events it created itself (each is marked with a hidden tag). It does not read your other events.

What the extension writes

Only class events in your own main Google Calendar: it adds your classes, updates them when ManageBac changes, and removes them when a class is cancelled or you click Remove all. It never changes or deletes events it did not create, never invites anyone, and adds no reminders.

What is stored, and where

  • On your computer only (Chrome's extension storage): your settings (words to skip, event colour, time zone, end of the school year), the progress of a sync so it can carry on if interrupted, and the calendar IDs of the classes added by the latest sync so Undo works.
  • Your Google sign-in is handled by Chrome itself. The extension never sees or stores your password and does not save the sign-in pass.
  • Your classes are stored in your Google Calendar, under your Google account, like any other event.

What leaves your computer

  • Requests to Google Calendar, sent directly from your computer to Google, to add, update or remove your class events.
  • Requests to ManageBac, to read your timetable weeks (the same as opening those pages yourself).
  • Bug reports, only if you send one. If you click Report a problem and press Send, the extension sends the developer your comment, the category you chose and technical details you can see before sending: extension and Chrome version, the address of the ManageBac page (which includes your school's ManageBac address), how the page was laid out (counts of rows and columns), time zones and language. It does not include your name, email, classes or a copy of the page. Reports are received through a Google Form owned by the developer and are used only to fix problems.

Nothing else is sent anywhere. There is no tracking, no analytics, no advertising, and nothing is sold or shared.

Google user data

The extension asks Google for one permission: See, create, change, and delete events on Google calendars you own (calendar.events.owned). It cannot see calendars other people share with you. It uses it only to add, update and remove the class events described above in your main calendar, at your request. Google data is used only to provide this feature, is never sent to the developer or anyone else, is not used for advertising, and no human reads it.

Timetable Sync for ManageBac's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Students

The extension is used by students and teachers at schools. The developer does not collect personal information from anyone, including students. A school's Google Workspace administrator decides whether its users may connect the extension to Google (see For school IT teams).

Removing your data

  • To remove the synced classes from your calendar: click the extension's toolbar button, then Remove all (tick the box to include classes that have already happened).
  • To disconnect Google: click Sign out in the toolbar button, or remove the extension's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Removing the extension from Chrome deletes its settings from your computer.
  • To ask about a bug report you sent, email hello@qurious.im.
