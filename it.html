For school IT teams

How to allow Timetable Sync for ManageBac for your staff and students in the Google Admin console. It takes a couple of minutes.

Why you're seeing this

Timetable Sync for ManageBac is a Chrome extension that copies a user's class timetable from ManageBac into their own Google Calendar. It signs in with Google and asks for one permission: Google Calendar events.

Many schools restrict third-party apps, and Google Workspace for Education blocks unconfigured third-party apps for users under 18 by default. Until an admin marks the app as Trusted, affected users see "Service has been disabled for this account" from Google, or the extension's message "Your school's Google admin hasn't allowed Timetable Sync for ManageBac to use Google Calendar yet."

Mark the app as Trusted

You need a Google Workspace super admin, or an admin with Security settings rights.

  1. Go to admin.google.com and sign in with your admin account.
  2. Open the menu (☰), then go to Security > Access and data control > API controls.
  3. Under App access control, click Manage Third-Party App Access.
  4. Click Configure new app.
  5. Paste the OAuth client ID above into the search box and click Search. Click Select next to Timetable Sync for ManageBac.
  6. Tick the client ID and click Select.
  7. Choose who it applies to: your whole domain, or the organizational units for your staff and students. Click Continue.
  8. Choose Trusted: Can access all Google services, click Continue, then Finish.

The change usually takes effect within minutes, and can take up to 24 hours. Users then sync with no further action from you.

If the app isn't found by its client ID, nobody in your domain has tried to sign in yet, and it may not appear in the list. Ask one staff member to click Sync to Google Calendar once (it will be blocked), then search again.

Optional: install it for everyone

To put the extension in users' Chrome without them installing it: in the Admin console go to Devices > Chrome > Apps & extensions > Users & browsers, choose the organizational unit, click the yellow + button > Add Chrome app or extension by ID, paste the Chrome extension ID above, and set it to Force install or Allow install. This is separate from the Trusted step: users still need the app to be Trusted to connect Google Calendar.

What the extension can and can't do

  • It runs entirely in the user's browser. There is no server, and the developer never receives timetables, calendars or account details.
  • It only adds, updates and removes the class events it created itself (tagged with a private extended property) in the user's primary calendar. It never reads, changes or deletes other events, never sends invitations, and adds no reminders.
  • It reads only the user's timetable pages on ManageBac, using their existing ManageBac session.
  • Users can remove all synced classes at any time with Remove all, and disconnect with Sign out.

Full details are in the privacy policy.

Before you've trusted it

Users can still use Download .ics on their ManageBac timetable page. It saves the timetable as a calendar file they can import into Google Calendar without connecting the extension. Imported classes don't update automatically.
